Use the private downloader with a channel or individual video you own or have permission to save.

Paste a channel or video URL

Use a channel link for its Videos or Shorts tab, or paste a watch, youtu.be, or Shorts link for one item.

Select the content

Choose Videos or Shorts, then pick Original, 4:5, 1:1, or 5:4 framing and preview the channel.

Save the ZIP to your device

Start the download. Your browser receives a ZIP containing the MP4 files in its Downloads folder.

Common questions

Does it download videos to the hosting server?

The app streams video data through the VPS to create the ZIP response for your browser. It does not save the video files or completed ZIP as a server-side library. The VPS still uses bandwidth and temporary runtime resources while a transfer is active.

Will every video download in 4K?

No. The selected quality is the highest available for each source. A 4K file is produced only when the video provides a 4K stream. Otherwise, the best available resolution is used.

Can I crop a video to 4:5, 1:1, or 5:4?

Yes. Choose a centered 4:5 portrait crop, a 1:1 square crop, or a 5:4 landscape crop. Cropping changes the frame and re-encodes the video, so it can take longer than keeping the original frame.

Can I download any YouTube channel?

Use this tool only for videos you own or are authorized to save. You are responsible for respecting copyright, permissions, and YouTube’s terms.

Can I select just one video or Short?

Yes. Paste its YouTube watch, youtu.be, or Shorts URL, then choose Download selection. Preview is optional, and only that linked item is added to the ZIP.

Respect creators’ rights. Only download content you own or have permission to save. This app does not bypass DRM or use account cookies.
